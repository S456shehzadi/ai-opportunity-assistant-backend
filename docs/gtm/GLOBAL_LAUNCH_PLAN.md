# Global Go-to-Market (GTM) Launch Plan

**Product:** AI Opportunity Assistant  
**Target Launch:** Q4 2026  
**Audience Scope:** Global (International Students, Early-Career Researchers, Fellows, Grant Applicants)  
**Last Updated:** October 10, 2026  

---

## 1. Product Positioning & Value Proposition

### Positioning Statement
> "For ambitious students, scholars, researchers, and early-career changemakers worldwide who are overwhelmed by fragmented scholarship portals, unreliable directories, and missed deadlines, **AI Opportunity Assistant** is the grounded, intelligent discovery companion that searches official sources in real time, evaluates complex eligibility criteria against your academic profile, and tracks deadlines—guaranteeing zero fabricated claims and connecting you directly to verified application links."

### Core Differentiators
1. **Verified Official Sources:** Direct indexing and retrieval of official universities, government departments (e.g. DAAD, Chevening, Fulbright), and reputable foundations—not ad-cluttered scrape farms.
2. **Deterministic Eligibility Veto:** Algorithms evaluate academic prerequisites, GPA conversions, and language test requirements without hallucinating eligibility.
3. **Transparent Advisory Framing:** Explicitly clarifies that matching is informational and directs users to official portals to apply.
4. **Privacy-by-Design:** No advertising tracking, no data resale, and immediate right-to-be-forgotten account purging.

## 2. Target Audience Segments

1. **Undergraduate & Graduate Applicants:** Searching for bachelor's, master's, or doctoral funding, particularly international students navigating visa restrictions and language testing waivers.
2. **Postdoctoral Researchers & Academics:** Seeking research grants, visiting fellowships, and laboratory stipends.
3. **Emerging Founders & Changemakers:** Looking for social impact grants, startup incubators, and global exchange programs.
