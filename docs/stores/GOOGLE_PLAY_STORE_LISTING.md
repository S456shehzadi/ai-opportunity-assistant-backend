### Full Description (Max 4000 characters)
```text
Discover scholarships, research fellowships, study grants, and career-accelerating funding worldwide with AI Opportunity Assistant.

Finding real funding for higher education, research, or startup ventures shouldn't mean navigating broken links, outdated directories, or misleading claims. AI Opportunity Assistant is designed to help students, researchers, professionals, and founders find verified, high-impact opportunities directly from official sources.

KEY FEATURES:

• GROUNDED OPPORTUNITY SEARCH
Search thousands of academic programs, scholarships, fellowships, and grants in natural language. Every opportunity links directly to the official host institution, foundation, or government website.

• PERSONALIZED ELIGIBILITY CHECK
Compare your academic background, degree levels, target disciplines, and language proficiencies against stated requirements. Understand your potential fit before spending hours preparing applications.

• DEADLINE ALERTS & TRACKING
Save promising opportunities to your personal dashboard. Receive timely reminders as deadlines approach so you never miss an application cycle.

• CONVERSATIONAL AI ASSISTANT
Ask questions about program prerequisites, eligible nationalities, funding stipends, and document checklists. All answers are grounded strictly in verified source materials.

• PRIVACY-FIRST BY DESIGN
No advertisements. No sale of personal data. You maintain complete control over your academic profile, and you can delete your account and records at any time.

IMPORTANT ADVISORY DISCLAIMER:
AI Opportunity Assistant is an independent informational and search assistance tool. The application DOES NOT guarantee admission to any academic program, award of any scholarship, or selection for any grant or fellowship. Opportunity requirements and deadlines change frequently; users must independently verify all details on the official host organization's website before applying.
```
